Before you call

How do I know I need an electrician?

Breakers that trip, outlets that die, lights that flicker, a burning smell, or a job that needs a new circuit. If you smell burning, get out and call.

What should I do while I wait?

If something is sparking or hot, kill that breaker. Don’t keep resetting a breaker that trips twice.
