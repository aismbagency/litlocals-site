The river wards are a made-up district for this draft. There is no license, no yard, no real mill. If you found this page on a preview host, you are looking at a pattern, not a plumber who will roll to your street.

A live area page should do three things: name the places they actually drive, say how far they will go for a night call, and tell a reader on the edge to ask. It should not scrape a rival’s city list or paste a Google rating they do not have.

What to say

Ward, street, nearest cross — even in a sample. A live shop learns in one sentence whether they cover you. Do not make them open a map app while water runs.

How a live map should work

Write the no as clearly as the yes

Shops get in trouble when the site implies the whole county. This sample lists a ward we will not cover so the pattern includes refusal. A homeowner on the edge should see “ask” instead of a pin that lies.

Travel time is the other honest line. Night calls shrink. Weather shrinks. A page that says “we come everywhere” trains people to wait on a porch in a place you will not drive.

Do not copy a competitor’s city list. Do not name a real Los Angeles neighborhood to look local. If your shop is real, use your streets. If this is still a sample, keep the fiction labeled.
