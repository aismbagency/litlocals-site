What to say when you call

The name of the shop. The street — in this sample, a river-wards block, which is fictional. Whether guests are in the room. Whether you can shut a fixture and still run service. Whether this happened last week too.

A live plumber will tell you if they take commercial at all. This page exists so that answer is easy to give: yes, small kitchens and restrooms; no, we do not pretend to be a plant crew.

Hours that match the work

Desk hours on this sample are weekday-heavy. A real commercial page often adds a close-of-service window. Steal that idea. Do not steal a competitor’s account list.
