The tell

When it is not “just a clog”

Gurgling in a bath when the washer drains. A smell in the lowest room. Paper that comes back. A cleanout that weeps. Those are line words. The sample shop puts them here so a homeowner does not keep plunging the same toilet.

Stop using the fixtures if it is coming up. That is the emergency split. If it is slow but contained, book the look. Either way, say how many fixtures, and whether this has happened after rain — in a real town that can mean a city main. In this sample town it is only a question to ask, not a claim about a municipal map.

The work

Look, then the cable, then the talk

A camera is not a product grid. It is how you stop paying for a guess. This sample does not invent a footage price or a “rooter special.” A live shop writes what they charge after they see the line.

City vs private: a website should not pretend to know the cleanout on a street it invented. The river wards are fictional. The useful line is: “we will tell you if this looks like your pipe or something past it.”
