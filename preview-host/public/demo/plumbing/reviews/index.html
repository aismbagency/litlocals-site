These lines were written for the draft. They are not from a review site, not from a Land-book still, and not from a living crew. A shop that buys this replaces them with words their own people actually heard — or they leave the page quieter. Silence is better than a borrowed name.

Why there are no stars

A star row is easy to fake and easy to steal. A sentence is harder. This page is built so a live shop can drop in three things a neighbor actually said — or leave the page with one honest line and a caption that it is a sample.

We will not load a widget, will not claim a rating, and will not paste a name that belongs to a real person in a real city.

How a shop should replace these

Ask the last five people who called. Write the sentence they used, not a paragraph from a marketing list. Mark anything still invented as sample until it is real. If you do not have words yet, keep the emergency split and the phone — those do more work than a carousel.
