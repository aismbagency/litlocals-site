What we ask on the call

Which fixture. How long. Whether a plunger moved it. Whether a cleanout is in the yard. Whether this has happened before on the same line. A live shop uses those answers to decide cable, camera, or a later slot.

This sample does not sell a brand of machine. It sells the habit of saying “we look, then we talk.” If a camera is the honest next step, that belongs in the driveway sentence, not as a menu upsell.
