Before you call

How do I know I need an HVAC tech?

No cool air, no heat, ice on the lines, a burning smell, or a unit that short-cycles. If you’re not sure, call and describe it.

What should I do while I wait?

Check the thermostat batteries and the breaker. Change a filthy filter if you can reach it. Don’t keep resetting a unit that trips.
